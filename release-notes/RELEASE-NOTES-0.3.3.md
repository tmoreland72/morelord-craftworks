# Morelord Craftworks 0.3.3

Morelord Craftworks 0.3.3 makes standard content available in new worlds without requiring a Morelord account connection.

## Fixed

- Removed account authentication requirements from the non-premium **Craftworks Standard** and **SRD 5.2** content packs.
- New worlds can now index standard materials and enabled SRD 5.2 recipes immediately after content synchronization.

## Notes

- Authentication and entitlement checks remain required for premium content packs and premium crafting features.
- Foundry VTT v14 and D&D5e 5.3+ remain required.
