# Third-Party Content Attribution

Morelord Craftworks includes structured crafting data adapted from **Kibbles’ Compendium of Craft and Creation**, version 1.1.3.

Kibbles’ Compendium of Craft and Creation Copyright 2021, KibblesTasty.

The Standard content pack uses table-level crafting facts such as material names, recipe names, component requirements, crafting times, check counts, difficulties, rarities, and values. Descriptive item prose and artwork from the source book are not bundled in this module.

Open Game Content included under the Open Game License v1.0a is distributed subject to the license included in `OPEN-GAME-LICENSE-1.0a.txt`.

## Drakkenheim material definitions and family icons

The Monsters of Drakkenheim content pack incorporates ingredient naming data
and family/rarity icon assets supplied from Antics & Rolls: Drakkenheim
Mastercraft v1.1.0 by GabeAntics. The upstream module states that its ingredient
family icons are sourced from game-icons.net.

Monsters of Drakkenheim item descriptions and commercial compendium documents
are not redistributed by Morelord Craftworks. Craftworks resolves finished
recipe outputs from a user's separately installed official `drakkenheim-monsters`
module at runtime.
