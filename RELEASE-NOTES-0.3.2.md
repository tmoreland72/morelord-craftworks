# Morelord Craftworks 0.3.2

Morelord Craftworks 0.3.2 fixes manual content synchronization from the module settings window.

## Fixed

- Fixed **Sync with Compendiums** failing with `craftworks is not defined`, preventing materials and recipes from being imported or refreshed in a new world.

## Notes

- Foundry VTT v14 and D&D5e 5.3+ remain required.
