# Morelord Craftworks — product demo narration

The timestamps target `MorelordCraftworks-Demo-Branded-TownTheme-v1.mp4`.

## 00:00–00:05 — Opening

Morelord Craftworks turns encounters, exploration, and monster trophies into a complete crafting economy for Foundry VTT.

## 00:05–00:20 — Configuration

Game Masters choose which Craftworks features are available, configure acquisition rules, and control the compendium sources used throughout the module.

## 00:20–01:04 — Harvesting

Harvest creatures directly from the scene or create gathering opportunities for the party. Craftworks calculates the available materials, presents the appropriate checks, tracks progress, and awards the results to the chosen character or shared party inventory.

## 01:04–01:26 — Encounter loot

Encounter Loot turns a completed fight into rewards in seconds. Select the defeated creatures, review the generated materials and treasure, then award everything through a clear, traceable workflow.

## 01:26–02:12 — Materials and recipes

The material catalog brings enabled sources into one searchable library. Recipes connect those ingredients to real compendium items, with configurable quantities, tools, abilities, difficulty, crafting time, and outcome rules.

## 02:12–02:40 — Crafting

When a character begins crafting, Craftworks checks their available materials and requirements. Successful work consumes the ingredients, creates the finished item, and records the result for the table.

## 02:40–03:08 — Spell scrolls

The Spell Scroll Generator creates random scroll rewards by spell level and awards usable D&D5e scroll items directly to a character or group.

## 03:08–03:26 — Potions

The Potion Generator builds rarity-based treasure packages from enabled item compendiums, then groups and awards the selected results in one action.

## 03:26–03:53 — Spellbooks

Recovered spellbooks can contain any chosen mix of spell levels. Generate a unique collection, reroll it until it fits the adventure, and create a linked spellbook for the recipient.

## 03:53–03:59 — Closing

Morelord Craftworks. Make every victory matter.
